## Installation

```bash
pnpm add @wondesign/loading
```

<Callout variant="warning" title="Requires React 19">
  `@wondesign/loading` needs React 19 or later.
</Callout>

---

## Key Features

- ✅ **Ready to Animate**: Every component animates on its own, with no setup
- ✅ **Fits Its Surroundings**: Spinners take the color of the text around them
- ✅ **Placeholder Shapes**: Skeletons for lines of text, avatars and cards
- ✅ **Styleable**: Your `className` and `style` are applied on top of the defaults

---

## Components

All of the components are styled based on `@wondesign/tokens`.

- ⭐ **[Spinner](/components/loading/spinner)** — a spinning icon, for an
  action in progress, in four styles.
- ⭐ **[Skeleton](/components/loading/skeleton)** — a placeholder in the
  shape of the content that's loading.

---

## Upcoming Features

- 🔜 **Progress Bar**: Show how far along an action is, for uploads and downloads
- 🔜 **Progress Ring**: A ring that fills up to show how far along an action is, in the space of a spinner
- 🔜 **Loading Overlay**: Cover a section with a spinner while its content is loading
