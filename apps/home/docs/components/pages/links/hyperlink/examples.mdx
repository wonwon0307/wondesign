# Examples

Common ways to use `Hyperlink`, from picking a color to fitting it into your
own design. Each example shows the rendered result, followed by its code.
**Every link on this page takes you to the top of the page.**

---

## Appearances

The `appearance` property sets the color. `"default"` is the default:

- 🎨 **`"default"`**: the classic link color, for links in body text.
- 🎨 **`"primary"`**: the primary color, to match the rest of your design.
- 🎨 **`"muted"`**: the muted text color, for less important links, like in
  a footer.
- 🎨 **`"inherit"`**: the color of the text around it, for colored
  surfaces like banners and dark footers.

<Preview>
  <p>
    {"Read our "}
    <Hyperlink href="#">{"privacy policy"}</Hyperlink>
    {" before you sign up."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"New here? "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" appearance="primary">{"Create an account"}</Hyperlink>
    {" to get started."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"Something wrong? "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" appearance="muted">{"Contact support"}</Hyperlink>
    {"."}
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="/privacy">privacy policy</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="/signup" appearance="primary">Create an account</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="/support" appearance="muted">Contact support</Hyperlink>
```

Use `"inherit"` on a colored surface, like an error banner. It's always
underlined, since the underline is the only thing that shows it's a link:

<Preview>
  <p
    style={{
      padding: "var(--spacing-md)",
      borderRadius: "var(--radius-sm)",
      color: "var(--color-text-inverted)",
      backgroundColor: "var(--color-error)",
    }}
  >
    {"Payment failed. "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" appearance="inherit">{"Update your card"}</Hyperlink>
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="/billing" appearance="inherit">Update your card</Hyperlink>
```

- 📌 **`underline` is ignored with `"inherit"`.**

---

## Underline

The `underline` property sets when the link is underlined. `"hover"` is the
default:

- 📏 **`"hover"`**: underlined while the pointer is over it.
- 📏 **`"always"`**: always underlined, for links within body text.
- 📏 **`"none"`**: never underlined, for links that are clearly links, like
  in a navigation or a footer.

<Preview>
  <p>
    {"See the "}
    <Hyperlink href="#">{"installation guide"}</Hyperlink>
    {" to set it up."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"The "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" underline="always">{"release notes"}</Hyperlink>
    {" list every change in this version."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"Follow us on "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" underline="none">{"GitHub"}</Hyperlink>
    {"."}
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="/install">installation guide</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="/changelog" underline="always">release notes</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="https://github.com/wonwon0307" underline="none">GitHub</Hyperlink>
```

## Disabled

Pass `isDisabled` to turn the link off. It can't be followed, leaves the tab
order, fades, and isn't underlined on hover:

<Preview>
  <p>
    {"Your invoices are in "}
    <Hyperlink href="#">{"Billing"}</Hyperlink>
    {"."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"Your invoices are in "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" isDisabled>{"Billing"}</Hyperlink>
    {", once your plan is active."}
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="/billing">Billing</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="/billing" isDisabled>Billing</Hyperlink>
```

## Styling

When a single link needs to look different, change it with `className` or
`style`, like a lighter font weight:

<Preview>
  <p>
    {"Read the "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" style={{ fontWeight: "normal" }}>{"full article"}</Hyperlink>
    {" for more."}
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="/posts/42" style={{ fontWeight: "normal" }}>
  full article
</Hyperlink>
```
