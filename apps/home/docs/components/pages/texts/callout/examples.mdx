# Examples

Common ways to use `Callout`, from picking a variant to fitting it into your
own design. Each example shows the rendered result, followed by its code.

---

## Variants

The `variant` property sets the colors and the default icon. It's `"info"` by
default.

Each variant colors the text and the icon, and tints the background using the
[`colorWithFade`](/foundations/tokens/utilities/colors#colorwithfade) utility
from `@wondesign/tokens`.

### Info

Uses the `tokens.colors.text` token. Use it for something worth knowing,
like how billing works:

<Preview>
  <Callout variant="info">
    {"Invoices are created on the 1st of every month."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout variant="info">
  Invoices are created on the 1st of every month.
</Callout>
```

### Warning

Uses the `tokens.colors.warning` token. Use it for something to be careful
about, like a setting with side effects:

<Preview>
  <Callout variant="warning">
    {
      "Changing your username also changes your profile URL. Links to the old URL will stop working."
    }
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout variant="warning">
  Changing your username also changes your profile URL. Links to the old URL
  will stop working.
</Callout>
```

### Error

Uses the `tokens.colors.error` token. Use it for something that's wrong and
needs fixing, like an expired card:

<Preview>
  <Callout variant="error">
    {"Your card has expired. Update it to keep using paid features."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout variant="error">
  Your card has expired. Update it to keep using paid features.
</Callout>
```

### Success

Uses the `tokens.colors.success` token. Use it for something that's set up
correctly, like a verified domain:

<Preview>
  <Callout variant="success">
    {"Your domain is verified. Emails are sent from @acme.com."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout variant="success">
  Your domain is verified. Emails are sent from @acme.com.
</Callout>
```

## Basic Usage

Pass text as the `title` and the content, and an icon's name from
[`@wondesign/icons`](/components/icons#icon-list) as the `icon`. The content is passed as `children`, between
the callout's opening and closing tags. The callout styles each of them for
you: the
title renders as a `Heading`, the content as a `Paragraph`, and the icon as
that icon. Leave out the `title` for a short note, and the `icon` to keep the
variant's own:

<Preview>
  <Callout variant="warning">
    {"Changing your username also changes your profile URL."}
  </Callout>
  <Callout variant="warning" title="Username" icon="palette">
    {"Changing your username also changes your profile URL."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
// without a title, with the variant's own icon
<Callout variant="warning">
  Changing your username also changes your profile URL.
</Callout>

// with a title and another icon
<Callout variant="warning" title="Username" icon="palette">
  Changing your username also changes your profile URL.
</Callout>
```

The `size` property sets the size of all three together. It's `"medium"` by
default:

- **`"large"`**: a level 4 title, large content and a large icon
- **`"medium"`**: a level 5 title, medium content and a medium icon
- **`"small"`**: a level 6 title, small content and a small icon

<Preview>
  <Callout size="large" title="Large">
    {"A level 4 title, with large content."}
  </Callout>
  <Callout size="medium" title="Medium">
    {"A level 5 title, with medium content."}
  </Callout>
  <Callout size="small" title="Small">
    {"A level 6 title, with small content."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout size="large" title="Large">
  A level 4 title, with large content.
</Callout>
<Callout size="medium" title="Medium">
  A level 5 title, with medium content.
</Callout>
<Callout size="small" title="Small">
  A level 6 title, with small content.
</Callout>
```

- 📌 **A text title renders as a heading**, an `<h4>`, `<h5>` or `<h6>`
  depending on the `size`, so it shows up in the page's outline.
- 📌 **Don't write long titles.** A callout's title is a short label for the
  note, and a text title is cut off after one line.

The default icons are as follows:

- 📍 **`"info"`**: `info`
- 📍 **`"warning"`**: `warning`
- 📍 **`"error"`**: `alert`
- 📍 **`"success"`**: `check-fill`

## Customizing

### Styling

When a callout needs to look different, change it with `className` or
`style`, like a flat banner with a colored edge:

<Preview>
  <Callout
    variant="success"
    style={{ borderRadius: 0, borderLeft: "4px solid" }}
  >
    {"Your domain is verified. Emails are sent from @acme.com."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout variant="success" style={{ borderRadius: 0, borderLeft: "4px solid" }}>
  Your domain is verified. Emails are sent from @acme.com.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="success" className="banner">
  Your domain is verified. Emails are sent from @acme.com.
</Callout>
```

```css
.banner {
  border-radius: 0;
  border-left: 4px solid;
}
```

- 📌 **`className` and `style` apply to the callout's box only.** To style the
  title, the content or the icon, pass them as your own components.

### Custom Components

Pass your own components as the `title`, the content or the `icon`, to
choose how each of them renders:

- 📍 **Title**: like a plain `<div>` that stays out of the page's outline
- 📍 **Content**: passed as `children`, like text with a link or a list
- 📍 **Icon**: like an emoji, or an icon from any icon library

**They render as they are, without the callout's styles for them.** Use the
text components yourself to keep the text styles:

<Preview>
  <Callout
    title={<div style={{ margin: "12px 0" }}>Need help?</div>}
    icon={<span>🎉</span>}
  >
    <Paragraph>
      {
        <>
          Read the <a href="/components/texts">guides</a>, or contact support.
        </>
      }
    </Paragraph>
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout
  title={<div style={{ margin: "12px 0" }}>Need help?</div>}
  // or an icon from any icon library, like lucide-react's <Rocket size="1em" />
  icon={<span>🎉</span>}
>
  <Paragraph>
    Read the <a href="/guides">guides</a>, or contact support.
  </Paragraph>
</Callout>
```

- 📌 Size your own icon at `1em` to follow the callout's `size`.
