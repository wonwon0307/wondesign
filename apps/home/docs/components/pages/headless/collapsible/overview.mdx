# Collapsible

`Collapsible` pairs a toggle with a section of content it shows and hides,
following the
[WAI-ARIA Disclosure pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/).
It wires the two together for assistive technology, so you only bring the look.

Use it to tuck away content that not everyone needs, like the details of an
order summary, advanced options in a form, or the answer to each question in
an FAQ.

---

## Key Features

- ✅ **Accessible by Default**: The toggle and content are linked for assistive technology
- ✅ **Keyboard Support**: Toggle the content with <Keyboard>Enter</Keyboard> or <Keyboard>Space</Keyboard>, like any button
- ✅ **Controlled & Uncontrolled**: Let it manage its own state, or drive it yourself
- ✅ **Disabled Toggle**: Turn off the toggle without removing it
- ✅ **Keep Content Mounted**: Hide closed content instead of removing it
- ✅ **Composable**: Render your own elements with [`asChild`](/components/headless/as-child)

## Structure

For what you can configure, refer to the
[Usage tab](/components/headless/collapsible/usage). For every prop, refer to
the [API tab](/components/headless/collapsible/api).

- 🧩 **Collapsible**: coordinates every part. It holds the open state, links
  the toggle to its content, and shares settings like `keepMounted`. **Renders
  no element of its own.**
- 🧩 **Collapsible.Toggle**: the button that opens and closes the content.
- 🧩 **Collapsible.Content**: the section that's shown and hidden.

```tsx
<Collapsible>
  <Collapsible.Toggle>Show details</Collapsible.Toggle>
  <Collapsible.Content>…</Collapsible.Content>
</Collapsible>;

// or, with named exports
<CollapsibleProvider>
  <CollapsibleToggle>Show details</CollapsibleToggle>
  <CollapsibleContent>…</CollapsibleContent>
</CollapsibleProvider>;
```

## How It Works

A disclosure has to tell the user whether its content is shown, and let them
change that from the keyboard. `Collapsible` builds this in, following the
[WAI-ARIA Disclosure pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/):
it decides how the content opens and closes, and what screen readers announce.
You don't need to wire up any of it.

### Toggling

The toggle is a button, so a click, <Keyboard>Enter</Keyboard> or

<Keyboard>Space</Keyboard> **opens the content when it's closed, and closes it
when it's open**. Nothing else opens or closes it.

### Screen Readers

The toggle is announced as expanded or collapsed, and points screen readers to
the content it controls, **but only while the content is on the page**. The
content is announced as a group of related items.
