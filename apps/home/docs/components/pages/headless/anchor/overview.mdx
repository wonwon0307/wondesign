# Anchor

`Anchor` renders a plain `<a>` and accepts all of its props. It knows when to
open a new tab, can be disabled, and works with your router's link component.

Any `href` starting with `http://`, `https://` or `//` counts as external, and
opens in a new tab, safely, so the new page can't reach back into yours. This
**includes full URLs to your own site**. Every other `href`, like `/docs`,
`#faq` or `mailto:`, opens in the current tab.

A disabled link loses its `href`, can't be clicked, and **leaves the tab
order**. It also renders a plain `<a>` even if you passed `as`, so your router
can't navigate.

```tsx
import { Anchor } from "@wondesign/headless/Anchor";

<Anchor href="/pricing">Pricing</Anchor>;
```

For ready-made styled links, use [`@wondesign/links`](/components/links),
which builds on `Anchor`.

---

## Key Features

- ✅ **External Links Handled**: Links to other sites open in a new tab, safely
- ✅ **Disabled State**: A link that can't be followed or focused
- ✅ **Router Friendly**: Render your router's link component with `as`
- ✅ **Styleable State**: Disabled, external and new-tab links are exposed as `data-*` attributes
- ✅ **Ref Forwarding**: Your `ref` reaches the rendered element

For what you can configure, refer to the
[Usage tab](/components/headless/anchor/usage). For every prop, refer to the
[API tab](/components/headless/anchor/api).
