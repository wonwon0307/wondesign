# Popover

`Popover` shows floating content next to its trigger, as a non-modal dialog,
following the
[WAI-ARIA Dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/)
without the parts that make it modal. It handles opening and closing, moves
focus in and back out, works out where the content fits on screen, and labels
it for assistive technology, so you only bring the look.

Use it when the content belongs next to its trigger, like a filter form or
quick settings. The page behind **stays usable** while it's open.

Don't use it when the content doesn't need to sit next to its trigger, like a
long form or a step the user has to finish before carrying on. For a short hint
with nothing to interact with, use a [Tooltip](/components/headless/tooltip)
instead.

---

## Key Features

- ✅ **Accessible by Default**: Announced as a dialog, named by its title
- ✅ **Closes On**: A close button, <Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard> or a click outside
- ✅ **Focus Management**: Moves focus in when it opens, and returns it to the trigger when it closes
- ✅ **Non-Modal**: The page behind stays usable while it's open
- ✅ **Auto Flip & Shift**: Flips to the opposite side when space runs short, and shifts to stay inside the viewport
- ✅ **Controlled & Uncontrolled**: Let it manage the open state, or drive it yourself
- ✅ **Keep Content Mounted**: Hide closed content instead of removing it
- ✅ **Composable**: Render your own elements with [`asChild`](/components/headless/as-child)

## Structure

For what you can configure, refer to the
[Usage tab](/components/headless/popover/usage). For every prop, refer to the
[API tab](/components/headless/popover/api).

- 🧩 **Popover**: coordinates every part. It holds the open state, handles
  dismissal and focus, and works out the position. **Renders no element of its
  own.**
- 🧩 **Popover.Trigger**: the button that opens the popover.
- 🧩 **Popover.Content**: the floating dialog itself.
- 🧩 **Popover.Title**: the heading that names the popover.
- 🧩 **Popover.Close**: a button that closes the popover, after running its
  `onClick`.
- 🧩 **Popover.Arrow**: an optional arrow pointing at the trigger.

```tsx
<Popover>
  <Popover.Trigger>Filters</Popover.Trigger>
  <Popover.Content>
    <Popover.Title>Filters</Popover.Title>
    {/* … */}
    <Popover.Close>Done</Popover.Close>
    <Popover.Arrow>…</Popover.Arrow>
  </Popover.Content>
</Popover>;

// or, with named exports
<PopoverProvider>
  <PopoverTrigger>Filters</PopoverTrigger>
  <PopoverContent>
    <PopoverTitle>Filters</PopoverTitle>
    {/* … */}
    <PopoverClose>Done</PopoverClose>
    <PopoverArrow>…</PopoverArrow>
  </PopoverContent>
</PopoverProvider>;
```

## How It Works

A popover has to open where the user expects it, take focus without trapping
it, and get out of the way when the user is done. `Popover` builds this in,
following the
[WAI-ARIA Dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/)
without the parts that make it modal: it decides how the popover closes, where
focus goes, where the content sits, and what screen readers announce. You don't
need to wire up any of it.

### Opening and Closing

A click on `Popover.Trigger` opens the popover. It closes through
`Popover.Close`, a click outside, or <Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard>.

<Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard> only closes the popover **while focus is inside it**.
Once focus has moved elsewhere on the page, <Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard> is left to
whatever has focus.

Clicks outside are handled by
[Click Outside](/foundations/pointer/click-outside),
and <Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard> by
[Escape Key](/foundations/key-events/escape-key).

### Focus

When the popover opens, focus moves to the **first focusable element inside the
content**. With nothing focusable inside, the content itself takes focus. When
it closes, focus returns to `Popover.Trigger`.

Focus isn't trapped: <Keyboard>Tab</Keyboard> and

<KeyboardGroup shortkey="Shift+Tab" /> can move out of the content, and the
popover **stays open** when they do.

Focus is handled by [Auto Focus](/foundations/focus/auto-focus).

### Placement

If the preferred side doesn't have room, the popover **flips to the opposite
side**, as long as that side has more room. It also shifts along its side to
stay inside the viewport, and follows the trigger when the page scrolls or
resizes. The position is worked out by
[Position](/foundations/position/floating-position), so it behaves exactly as
described there.

### Screen Readers

The trigger is announced as a button that opens a dialog, along with whether
it's open. The content is announced as a dialog, named by `Popover.Title`.
Without a title, it's named by its `aria-label`, "Popover Content" by default,
so **a title or an `aria-label` of your own is strongly recommended**.

## Known Issues

- 🐞 **Focus jumps back after a click outside**: Closing the popover by clicking
  elsewhere returns focus to the trigger, instead of leaving it where the user
  clicked.
- 🐞 **Nested overlays close the popover**: Clicking inside a popover, tooltip
  or other overlay opened from inside the popover counts as a click outside,
  and closes it.
