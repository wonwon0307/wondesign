# Modal

`Modal` shows content on top of the page and blocks the page behind until the
user is done, following the
[WAI-ARIA Dialog (Modal) pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/).
It handles opening and closing, moves and keeps focus inside, blocks the page
behind, and labels it for assistive technology, so you only bring the look.

Use it when the user has to finish a step before carrying on, like editing a
profile, filling in a long form, or reviewing details before a purchase.

Don't use it when the content belongs next to its trigger and the page should
stay usable, like a filter form. Use a [Popover](/components/headless/popover)
instead. For an urgent message that needs an answer, like confirming a
destructive action, use an [Alert](/components/headless/alert).

---

## Key Features

- ✅ **Accessible by Default**: Announced as a modal dialog, named by its title
- ✅ **Closes On**: A close button, <Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard> or a click outside
- ✅ **Focus Management**: Moves focus in when it opens, keeps it inside, and returns it when it closes
- ✅ **Blocks the Page Behind**: The page behind can't be focused or scrolled while the modal is open
- ✅ **Optional Backdrop**: Add a layer behind the modal, or leave it out
- ✅ **Controlled & Uncontrolled**: Let it manage the open state, or drive it yourself
- ✅ **Keep Content Mounted**: Hide closed content instead of removing it
- ✅ **Composable**: Render your own elements with [`asChild`](/components/headless/as-child)

## Structure

For what you can configure, refer to the
[Usage tab](/components/headless/modal/usage). For every prop, refer to the
[API tab](/components/headless/modal/api).

- 🧩 **Modal**: coordinates every part. It holds the open state, manages focus,
  and blocks the page behind while it's open. **Renders no element of its
  own.**
- 🧩 **Modal.Trigger**: the button that opens the modal, after running its
  `onClick`.
- 🧩 **Modal.Backdrop**: an optional layer behind the modal.
- 🧩 **Modal.Content**: the modal dialog itself.
- 🧩 **Modal.Title**: the heading that names the modal.
- 🧩 **Modal.Close**: a button that closes the modal, after running its
  `onClick`.

```tsx
<Modal>
  <Modal.Trigger>Edit profile</Modal.Trigger>
  <Modal.Backdrop />
  <Modal.Content>
    <Modal.Title>Edit profile</Modal.Title>
    {/* … */}
    <Modal.Close>Cancel</Modal.Close>
    <Modal.Close onClick={saveProfile}>Save</Modal.Close>
  </Modal.Content>
</Modal>;

// or, with named exports
<ModalProvider>
  <ModalTrigger>Edit profile</ModalTrigger>
  <ModalBackdrop />
  <ModalContent>
    <ModalTitle>Edit profile</ModalTitle>
    {/* … */}
    <ModalClose>Cancel</ModalClose>
    <ModalClose onClick={saveProfile}>Save</ModalClose>
  </ModalContent>
</ModalProvider>;
```

## How It Works

A modal has to hold the user's attention until they're done, without letting
them lose their place on the page behind. `Modal` builds this in, following the
[WAI-ARIA Dialog (Modal) pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/):
it decides how the modal closes, where focus goes, what happens to the page
behind, and what screen readers announce. You don't need to wire up any of it.

### Opening and Closing

A click on `Modal.Trigger` opens the modal. It closes through `Modal.Close`,
{<Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard>} and a click outside the content (both can be switched
off). A click on `Modal.Backdrop` counts as a click outside.

Clicks outside are handled by
[Click Outside](/foundations/pointer/click-outside), and
{<Keyboard>Esc</Keyboard>} by [Escape Key](/foundations/key-events/escape-key).

### Focus

When the modal opens, focus moves to the **first focusable element inside the
content**. With nothing focusable inside, the content itself takes focus.

While it's open, <Keyboard>Tab</Keyboard> and
{<KeyboardGroup shortkey="Shift+Tab" />} stay inside the content. When it closes,
focus returns to `Modal.Trigger`. If the trigger doesn't exist, focus returns to
whatever had it before the modal opened.

Focus is handled by [Auto Focus](/foundations/focus/auto-focus) and
[Focus Trap](/foundations/focus/focus-trap).

### The Page Behind

While the modal is open, everything outside the content can't be focused or
interacted with, and the page stops scrolling. These are handled by
[Background Inert](/foundations/focus/background-inert) and
[Scroll Lock](/foundations/disclosure/scroll-lock) respectively, so **their
known issues carry over to `Modal`**.

### Screen Readers

The trigger is announced as a button that opens a dialog. The content is
announced as a modal dialog, named by `Modal.Title`. Without a title, it's
named by its `aria-label`, "Modal" by default, so **a title or an
`aria-label` of your own is strongly recommended**.

## Known Issues

- 🐞 **Nested overlays close the modal**: Clicking inside a popover, tooltip or
  other overlay opened from inside the modal counts as a click outside, and
  closes it.
- 🐞 **Content added in the background while the modal is open**: It can still
  be clicked and focused.
- 🐞 **The page shifts sideways**: Where scrollbars take up space, the page
  shifts by the scrollbar's width while the modal is open.
