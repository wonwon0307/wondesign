# Interactions

Interactions are React hooks for the behaviour behind interactive components
like alerts, popovers and tabs: open state, focus, keyboard, pointer and
positioning. They hold **no markup and no styles** — attach them to your own
elements. Each package installs and works on its own.

Their logic is opinionated, but their style isn't. This makes them a **safe
dependency for any design system**, whatever its brand or styling approach.

---

## Packages

- 📦 **[`@wondesign/disclosure`](/interactions/disclosure)** — Open / closed state,
  closing animations and scroll locking for anything that shows and hides.
- 📦 **[`@wondesign/focus`](/interactions/focus)** — Arrow key navigation, focus
  on open and close, focus traps and an inert background.
- 📦 **[`@wondesign/key-events`](/interactions/key-events)** — Closing on Escape
  and cross-platform shortkeys like `Mod+K`.
- 📦 **[`@wondesign/pointer`](/interactions/pointer)** — Clicks outside an element
  and long presses, on mouse, touch and pen.
- 📦 **[`@wondesign/position`](/interactions/position)** — Placing floating
  content next to its trigger, keeping it on screen and pointing an arrow back
  at it.
