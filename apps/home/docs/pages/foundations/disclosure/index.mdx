<PackageInfo name="@wondesign/disclosure" relPath="foundations/disclosure">
  `@wondesign/disclosure` handles anything that shows and hides, like alerts,
  popovers and tooltips. It keeps track of whether it's open, keeps it on the
  page while it animates out, and locks the page's scroll while it's open. Use
  it straight in your app, or to build components of your own.
</PackageInfo>

---

## Installation

```bash
pnpm add @wondesign/disclosure
```

With [All-in-One](/all-in-one), import from `@wondesign/ui/disclosure`
instead.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Requires React 19">
  `@wondesign/disclosure` needs React 19 or later.
</Callout>

---

## Key Features

- ✅ **Controlled & Uncontrolled**: Let the state be kept for you, or pass it in yourself
- ✅ **Exit Animations**: Keep closed content on the page until its closing animation ends
- ✅ **Scroll Lock**: Stop the page behind open content from scrolling, even with several layers open

---

## Hooks

Each hook has its own entry, like `@wondesign/disclosure/open-state`, so you
only bundle the ones you use.

- ⭐ **[Open State](/foundations/disclosure/open-state)** — whether something
  is open, with ways to open, close and toggle it, controlled or uncontrolled.
- ⭐ **[Presence](/foundations/disclosure/presence)** — keeps closed content on
  the page until its closing animation ends.
- ⭐ **[Scroll Lock](/foundations/disclosure/scroll-lock)** — stops the page,
  or one element, from scrolling while something is open.
