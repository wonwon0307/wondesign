# Examples

This page explains what each prop of `Hyperlink` does. Each example shows the
rendered result, followed by its code. Clicking a link below takes you to the
top of the page.

---

## Appearance

The `appearance` property sets the color, with four options. Without it, the
link uses `"default"`.

`"default"` uses the classic blue link color. `"primary"` uses your primary
color, which is another shade of blue in this app, but can be any color in
yours. `"muted"` uses the muted text color, for less important links, like in a
footer:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <p>
    {"Read our "}
    <Hyperlink href="#">{"privacy policy"}</Hyperlink>
    {" before you sign up."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"New here? "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" appearance="primary">
      {"Create an account"}
    </Hyperlink>
    {" to get started."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"Something wrong? "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" appearance="muted">
      {"Contact support"}
    </Hyperlink>
    {"."}
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="#">privacy policy</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="#" appearance="primary">Create an account</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="#" appearance="muted">Contact support</Hyperlink>
```

The fourth option, `"inherit"`, takes the color of the text around it, for
colored surfaces like an error banner. It's always underlined, since the
underline is the only thing that shows it's a link:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <p
    style={{
      padding: "var(--spacing-md)",
      borderRadius: "var(--radius-sm)",
      color: "var(--color-text-inverted)",
      backgroundColor: "var(--color-error)",
    }}
  >
    {"Payment failed. "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" appearance="inherit">
      {"Update your card"}
    </Hyperlink>
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<p className="error-banner">
  Payment failed.{" "}
  <Hyperlink href="#" appearance="inherit">
    Update your card
  </Hyperlink>
</p>
```

- 📌 **`underline` is ignored with `"inherit"`.**

---

## Underline

The `underline` property sets when the link is underlined: while hovered,
always, or never, like in a navigation where links are clearly links.
`"hover"` is the default:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <p>
    {"See the "}
    <Hyperlink href="#">{"installation guide"}</Hyperlink>
    {" to set it up."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"The "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" underline="always">
      {"release notes"}
    </Hyperlink>
    {" list every change in this version."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"Follow us on "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" underline="none">
      {"GitHub"}
    </Hyperlink>
    {"."}
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="#">installation guide</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="#" underline="always">release notes</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="#" underline="none">GitHub</Hyperlink>
```

---

## Disabled

Pass `isDisabled` to turn the link off. Navigation is blocked, the link
fades, and it isn't underlined on hover:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <p>
    {"Your invoices are in "}
    <Hyperlink href="#">{"Billing"}</Hyperlink>
    {"."}
  </p>
  <p>
    {"Your invoices are in "}
    <Hyperlink href="#" isDisabled>
      {"Billing"}
    </Hyperlink>
    {", once your plan is active."}
  </p>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Hyperlink href="#">Billing</Hyperlink>
<Hyperlink href="#" isDisabled>Billing</Hyperlink>
```
