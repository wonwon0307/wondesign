import { Callout, Paragraph } from "@wondesign/ui/texts";
import { Preview } from "@/widgets/preview";

# Examples

This page explains what each prop of `Callout` does. Each example shows the
rendered result, followed by its code.

---

## Variants

The `variant` property sets the color and the icon. The options are
`"info"`, `"warning"`, `"error"` and `"success"`, and the default is
`"info"`. Their icons are `info`, `warning`, `alert` and `check-fill`:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <Callout>{"Invoices are created on the 1st of every month."}</Callout>
  <Callout variant="warning">
    {"Changing your username also changes your profile URL."}
  </Callout>
  <Callout variant="error">
    {"Your card has expired. Update it to keep using paid features."}
  </Callout>
  <Callout variant="success">
    {"Your domain is verified. Emails are sent from @acme.com."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout>Invoices are created on the 1st of every month.</Callout>
<Callout variant="warning">
  Changing your username also changes your profile URL.
</Callout>
<Callout variant="error">
  Your card has expired. Update it to keep using paid features.
</Callout>
<Callout variant="success">
  Your domain is verified. Emails are sent from @acme.com.
</Callout>
```

## Content

Pass text for a quick callout, or your own components to choose how each
part renders.

### Basic Usage

Pass the message as `children`. Add a `title` above it, or leave it out for a
short note. Pass an icon name from
[`@wondesign/icons`](/components/icons/app-icon/overview#icon-list) as
`icon` to replace the variant's own:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <Callout>{"The simplest usage, with no title."}</Callout>
  <Callout title="Callout with title and icon" icon="palette">
    {"A simple usage, with a title and a WonDesign icon in place of the variant's own."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout>The simplest usage, with no title.</Callout>
<Callout title="Callout with title and icon" icon="palette">
  A simple usage, with a title and a WonDesign icon in place of the variant's
  own.
</Callout>
```

- 📌 **Don't write long titles.** A callout's title is a short label for the
  note, and a text title is cut off after one line.

### Your Own Content

Pass your own components as the `title`, the message or the `icon`, like a
plain `<div>` that stays out of the page's outline, text with a link, or an
emoji. **They render as they are, without the callout's styles for them.**
Use the text components yourself to keep the text styles:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <Callout
    title={<div style={{ margin: "12px 0" }}>Need help?</div>}
    icon={<span>🎉</span>}
  >
    <Paragraph>
      {"Read the "}
      <Hyperlink href="#">{"guides"}</Hyperlink>
      {", or contact support."}
    </Paragraph>
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
import { Hyperlink } from "@wondesign/links/Hyperlink";

<Callout
  title={<div>Need help?</div>}
  // or an icon from any icon library, like lucide-react's <Rocket size="1em" />
  icon={<span>🎉</span>}
>
  <Paragraph>
    Read the <Hyperlink href="/guides">guides</Hyperlink>, or contact support.
  </Paragraph>
</Callout>;
```

## Sizes

The `size` property sets the size of the icon, the title and the message
together. It's `"medium"` by default. A text title is always an `<h5>`, and
it looks one level larger with `"large"`, and one level smaller with
`"small"`:

<Preview orientation="vertical">
  <Callout size="large" title="Storage">
    {"You've used 80% of your storage."}
  </Callout>
  <Callout size="medium" title="Storage">
    {"You've used 80% of your storage."}
  </Callout>
  <Callout size="small" title="Storage">
    {"You've used 80% of your storage."}
  </Callout>
</Preview>

```tsx
<Callout size="large" title="Storage">You've used 80% of your storage.</Callout>
<Callout size="medium" title="Storage">You've used 80% of your storage.</Callout>
<Callout size="small" title="Storage">You've used 80% of your storage.</Callout>
```
