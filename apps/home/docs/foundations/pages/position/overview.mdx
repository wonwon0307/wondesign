<PackageInfo name="@wondesign/position" relPath="foundations/position">
  `@wondesign/position` places floating content next to the element that opens
  it — tooltips, popovers, menus, dropdowns. It picks a side with room, keeps
  the content on screen, and points an arrow back at the trigger.
</PackageInfo>

---

## Installation

```bash
pnpm add @wondesign/position
```

<Callout variant="warning" title="Requires React 19">
  `@wondesign/position` needs React 19 or later.
</Callout>

---

## Key Features

- ✅ **Any Side, Any Alignment**: Place content above, below or beside its trigger, lined up at the start, center or end
- ✅ **Flips When Needed**: Moves to the opposite side when there isn't room
- ✅ **Stays on Screen**: Keeps content inside the viewport, with space to spare at the edges
- ✅ **Arrow Included**: Points an arrow at the trigger, even when the content has moved to fit
- ✅ **Keeps Up**: Follows the trigger on scroll and resize, and when the trigger, content or arrow changes size
- ✅ **Typed Refs**: Refs ready to attach, typed for the elements you use
