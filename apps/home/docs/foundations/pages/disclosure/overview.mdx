<PackageInfo name="@wondesign/disclosure" relPath="foundations/disclosure">
  `@wondesign/disclosure` handles anything that shows and hides — menus, panels,
  tooltips, sidebars, dialogs. It keeps track of whether it's open, and what
  happens while it is, like locking the page's scroll. Use it straight in your
  app, or to build components of your own.

## Key Features

- ✅ **Uncontrolled Mode**: The default, easy-to-use mode — the state is kept for you, starting open or closed
- ✅ **Controlled Mode**: Full control — pass the state in, and decide what happens on every change
- ✅ **Same API Either Way**: `isOpen`, `show`, `hide` and `toggle` work the same in both modes
- ✅ **Built-in Toggle**: Open or close with a single call
- ✅ **Stable Callbacks**: `show` and `hide` are safe to pass down or use in effects
- ✅ **Scroll Lock**: Stop the page behind open content from scrolling, even with several layers open

---

## Upcoming Features

- 🔜 **No Repeat Calls**: `onOpenChange` is only called when the state actually changes
- 🔜 **Mode-Switch Warning**: A warning in development when a component switches between controlled and uncontrolled
- 🔜 **onShow / onHide**: Callbacks for when the component opens or closes
- 🔜 **Delayed Open State**: Open and close after a delay, like a tooltip on hover
- 🔜 **Exit Animations**: Keep closed content on the page until its closing animation ends
